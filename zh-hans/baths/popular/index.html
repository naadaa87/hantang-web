HANTANG TOP RATED

Bathhouses, spas and scrub shops visitors rate highly

11 places that score well with a large number of visitors on Google Maps, Kakao Map, Naver Map and booking sites. They carry the HANTANG Top Rated badge everywhere on this site.

How a place earns the badge

  • An average of 4.2 out of 5 or higher from at least 500 reviews on Google Maps or Kakao Map; or
  • 4.5 or higher from at least 100 reviews on a booking site foreign visitors use (Klook, Creatrip, Trazy, KKday, Tripadvisor); or
  • for places with no star ratings, 2,000 or more visitor reviews on Naver Map together with a rating of 4.0 or higher elsewhere.

The place must still be open, with recent reviews. We check by hand (last check: 2026-10) and again every six months. The badge cannot be bought.

Places appear in a new random order on every visit. Nobody pays for a higher spot.
