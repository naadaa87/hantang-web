FIRST-TIMER GUIDE

Your first tang, in six steps.

The whole ritual, from the shoe locker to the sleeping mat. Nothing assumed, nothing embarrassing — everyone in that room was a first-timer once.

STEP 1

Shoes off at the door

Shoe lockers come first, always. Keep the small key — you may trade it for your main locker key.

STEP 2

Pay & get your key

Entry is paid up front. Your wristband key opens your locker and tracks anything you buy inside.

STEP 3

Undress fully

Yes, fully. Bath floors are nude and separated by gender. Nobody looks — it is the least remarkable thing in the room.

STEP 4

Wash before you soak

The one golden rule. Scrub down at the seated showers before touching any tang.

STEP 5

Soak, sauna, repeat

Hot tang, cold tang, sauna, rest. Find your own rotation — locals call the feeling being “siwonhada”.

STEP 6

Jjimjilbang time

Uniform on, into the mixed lounge: kiln rooms, sikhye, baked eggs, and — if you booked it — a place to sleep.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED STRAIGHT

Before you ask Reddit.

Can I enter a Korean sauna with tattoos?+
It depends on the bathhouse — and that's exactly why every HANTANG listing states its policy up front: Tattoo OK, cover-up (rash guard or patch), or no. Filter by Tattoo OK and you'll never have the awkward door moment.
Do I really have to be fully naked?+
In the bath area, yes — it's gender-separated and completely normal there; nobody pays attention. In jjimjilbang lounge floors, everyone wears the cotton uniform you're given at entry, and those floors are mixed.
How much does it cost?+
Public bathhouses typically run about ₩8,000–12,000 for entry, jjimjilbangs a little more with the uniform included, and a seshin scrub is usually ₩20,000–35,000 on top. Every listing shows its real, checked price.
What exactly is seshin?+
A full-body exfoliating scrub done by a professional with the green “Italy towel”, after you've soaked for 15–20 minutes. It removes a visible layer of dead skin — strange for one minute, addictive after ten. Look for the Seshin badge on listings.
Can I sleep overnight in a jjimjilbang?+
In many, yes — it's a classic budget stay. Listings marked Sleep OK have overnight hours and sleeping rooms; a few close at midnight, so check the card before you plan on it.
