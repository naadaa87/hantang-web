title: Korea in winter: how to fit a hot bath into a cold-weather trip description: In a Korean winter the sun sets soon after 5 p.m. and mornings can fall below −10°C. Where a jjimjilbang or hot spring fits into the day, which free foot baths stay open, and what to plan around. tag: TRAVEL ---

The short answer: see the outdoor sights while it is light, and put the bath in the evening. In December the sun sets at about a quarter past five, which leaves a long, cold evening. A jjimjilbang (찜질방) or a hot-spring bath fills it with hot water, food and a heated floor. If you have a spare day, give it to a hot-spring town. Three things are different in winter: baths fill up on the coldest days, some free outdoor foot baths close or cut their hours, and some baths close for Seollal (설날), the Lunar New Year, which falls on 7 February in 2027.

How cold, and how dark

The Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute (KASI) almanac for December 2026 lists sunset between 17:13 and 17:23 on every day of the month. On the winter solstice, 22 December, sunrise is at 07:43 and sunset at 17:18. The page names no city, so allow a few minutes either way.

The Korea Meteorological Administration issues a cold-wave advisory (한파주의보) between October and April. One trigger is a morning low expected to stay at −12°C or below for two days or more; the warning level starts at −15°C. A sudden drop of 10°C or more from the previous morning can also set it off. The Korea Economic Daily reported a morning low of −11°C in Seoul on 2 January 2026, against a normal of −5.5°C for that date.

Put the bath after sunset

Palaces, markets and mountain trails need daylight. A 24-hour jjimjilbang does not, and it has no last entry to race against. Sparex Dongdaemun, opposite DDP, is one example: our listing (October 2026) has it open around the clock, at ₩14,000 for a day ticket and ₩16,000 from 20:00. You wash, soak, change into the house uniform, eat, and lie on the warm floor as long as you like. A late-December day in Seoul could run like this:

  • 07:45. Sunrise. Breakfast indoors while the air warms up.
  • 10:00–16:30. Outdoor sights, with a café or museum stop in the middle.
  • 17:15. Sunset. An early dinner.
  • 18:30 onward. The jjimjilbang, for two or three hours.

Our 24-hour list has more places, and the area pages sort bathhouses by the neighborhood you are staying in. If you plan to sleep there, read the overnight guide linked below first.

Give one day to a hot spring

Most Korean hot-spring bathing happens in large indoor halls, so the weather stops mattering once you are inside. Three spa towns can be reached by rail:

  • Onyang (온양), in Asan. Baths and inns cluster around Onyangoncheon Station on Seoul Metro Line 1.
  • Suanbo (수안보), in Chungju. Suanbo Oncheon Station opened on 30 November 2024. The Kyunghyang Shinmun reported in January 2026 that high-speed trains between Pangyo and Mungyeong stop there eight times a day, counting both directions. The station is outside the spa district: about four minutes by car, the paper says, and a traveler in its report took a taxi because the bus left a walk of over 20 minutes.
  • Dongnae (동래), in Busan. Hurshimchung, the big bath near Oncheonjang Station on subway Line 1, is open 05:30–22:00 (checked November 2026). Our article on it has the prices.

Open-air baths exist but are not the norm. The ones on our hot spring map are mostly swimsuit zones. Seokmodo Mineral Hot Spring on Ganghwa, for example, has outdoor pools facing the sea, and a swimsuit or rash guard is required (₩9,000 in 2026, closed Tuesdays, according to our map). We have not confirmed winter schedules for outdoor pools, so check before you travel.

Three things to plan around

  • Crowds on the coldest days. During the cold snap of early January 2026, the Korea Economic Daily described a bathhouse in Seoul's Seodaemun district handing out waiting tickets and a large jjimjilbang in Daechi-dong running short of mats. Go on a weekday, or early in the day, if you can.
  • Seollal. The Lunar New Year is Sunday, 7 February 2027, and with a substitute holiday the break runs from 6 to 9 February. Practice varies: by our prices and hours guide, Sparex stays open all year and Haeundae Oncheon Center in Busan closes. Confirm the day before.
  • The walk home. Dry your hair and body completely before you step outside. The Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency counted 334 cases of cold-related illness in the winter of 2024–2025; 80.2% were hypothermia and 74% happened outdoors. Its advice includes thin layers, a hat, gloves and a scarf, and changing out of wet clothes quickly.

Coming in from freezing air, do not head straight for the hottest pool. Our heat and health guide, linked below, starts with a short shower, keeps each hot soak to 10–15 minutes and says no alcohol in the two hours before. If you have a heart or blood-pressure condition, read it before you go.
