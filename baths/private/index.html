PRIVATE BATHS

Private baths, solo saunas and one-person scrub rooms

A newer kind of Korean bath: a room of your own for an hour or two — a sauna, a tub, or a scrub table — booked in advance. No shared nudity, which makes them an easy first step.

Places appear in a new random order on every visit. Nobody pays for a higher spot.
