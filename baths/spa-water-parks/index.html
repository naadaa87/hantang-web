SPA WATER PARKS

Spa water parks: warm pools in a swimsuit

Big indoor and outdoor pools of warm or hot-spring water, where you wear a swimsuit and can go in together, usually with a bathhouse and a jjimjilbang in the same building.

SPA WATER PARKS

How a spa park works

Most places have three parts: the pools (swimsuit, mixed), the baths (naked, men and women apart) and the jjimjilbang (clothes provided, mixed). Tickets are often sold for each part or as a combined pass, so check what yours includes.

The pools usually include a bade pool (바데풀), a warm pool with massage jets at different heights. Outdoor pools may close or cool down in winter, and some parks open their outdoor zones in summer only.

The city parks are in shopping malls and hotels; the hot-spring ones are mostly resorts in spa towns such as Asan, Deoksan and Icheon.

Kids, families, women and special situations →

Before you go

  • Bring a swimsuit. Swim caps and life jackets are often required, and most counters rent them.
  • Shower naked in the changing room before you put your swimsuit on, as in any Korean bath.
  • Weekends and school holidays are crowded; weekday mornings are calmest.
  • Many parks do not allow outside food, but most have a food court.
