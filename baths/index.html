      Words you will see

      Jjimjilbang (찜질방, also spelled jimjilbang): a bathhouse with heated rooms, a shared hall and space to sleep, often open 24 hours. Mogyoktang (목욕탕): a neighbourhood public bath. Hanjeungmak (한증막): a dome-shaped kiln sauna. Seshin (세신) or ttaemiri: Korean body scrub, done by an attendant. Oncheon (온천): hot spring.
